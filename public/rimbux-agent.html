RIMBUX — 60-second walkthrough

            
NEAR Hackathon 2026 | AI Security & Defense Track

          

        

        
      


      

        

          
System A: Intelligent Spending Control

          
Mutex-locked reservation ledger enforcing Max Per-Tx (5 NEAR), Rolling 24h Limit (15 NEAR), Lifetime Cap (50 NEAR), Per-Recipient Cap (8 NEAR), rate limits, and concurrent race protection.

        

        

          
System B: Deterministic Policy Engine

          
Evaluates all signals into a non-overridable ALLOW, REQUIRE_APPROVAL, or BLOCK verdict and issues a single-use HMAC-SHA256 authorization token.

        

        

          
System C: Sandboxed NEAR FunctionCall Wallet

          
Isolates the AI key to a FunctionCall access key scoped strictly to execute_agent_payment on rimbux-vault.rimbux-agent.testnet with 0 direct native transfer rights.

        

        

          
System D & E: Reputation & Explainable Scam Engine

          
Queries live NEAR Testnet JSON-RPC (view_account) + threat feeds (Unknown != Malicious) and produces 5-point explainable alerts for prompt injection and typosquats.
